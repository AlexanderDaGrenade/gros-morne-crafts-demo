Our Story

Two generations of Newfoundland craft, under one roof.

When our parents opened the doors in 1971, Gros Morne Crafts was the only craft shop on the Northern Peninsula, and one of the first in all of Newfoundland.

Over the decades we've built relationships with makers right across the island. That's why today we carry the biggest and best selection of quality handmade Newfoundland products in Gros Morne National Park, at the best prices.

The Oceanside Cottage

Private oceanfront · Rocky Harbour

Very private and nestled in the trees on the oceanfront. French doors open onto a raised balcony with ocean views, and a short trail leads down to a quiet beach where you can relax, beachcomb and watch those famous sunsets.
